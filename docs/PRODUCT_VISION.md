# Product Vision

## Mission

Help Filipino voters make more informed electoral decisions by making public political information easier to find, understand, verify, and compare.

## What Gabay ni Juan is

A civic information platform that organizes documented public records related to political figures, election participants, offices, affiliations, policy positions, public disclosures, legal proceedings, and source evidence.

## What Gabay ni Juan is not

- a campaign platform
- a candidate endorsement platform
- a politician rating system
- a prediction engine
- an election winner forecast
- a political social network
- an opinion/news aggregation feed

## Core voter questions

The product should help a voter answer:

- Who is this person?
- What office are they seeking or holding?
- What positions have they held before?
- What party or affiliation history is documented?
- What policies have they publicly supported or opposed?
- What legislation or executive actions are associated with them?
- What public disclosures are available?
- Are there documented legal proceedings, and what is their actual status?
- Where did this information come from?
- When was it last verified?

## 2028 positioning

Until official election records establish candidacy, the system must not describe a person as an official 2028 candidate merely because they are discussed publicly as a possible contender.

The application should model lifecycle states such as:

- PUBLIC_FIGURE
- CURRENT_OFFICIAL
- POTENTIAL_ASPIRANT
- PUBLICLY_DECLARED_ASPIRANT
- FILED_COC
- OFFICIAL_CANDIDATE
- WITHDRAWN
- DISQUALIFIED
- ELECTED
- NOT_ELECTED

Statuses must include effective dates and evidence.

## Long-term differentiators

1. Evidence attached to individual claims.
2. Transparent correction history.
3. Political affiliation timeline.
4. Office/election history.
5. Legal-case procedural timeline.
6. Asset/public-disclosure history.
7. Family/political relationship graph.
8. Neutral side-by-side fact comparison.
